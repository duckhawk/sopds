# Organising the library

Four things group books here, and they are deliberately not the same thing.
Which one you want depends on who it is for.

## Genres

Come with the files. Whoever produced the FB2 or EPUB chose them, following a
taxonomy nobody in this library had a say in. Useful, and entirely out of your
hands.

## The shelf

Automatic. Opening or downloading a book puts it on your shelf, which is a
record of what you have touched, newest first. Alongside it:

- **Status** — *To read*, *Reading*, *Finished*. Set it by hand, or let an
  e-reader set it: progress synced from a device moves a book to *Reading* and
  then to *Finished* on its own.
- **Rating** — one to five stars. Yours is yours; the average of everyone's is
  what orders **Top rated**.

The shelf is private.

## Tags

Shared. A tag is whatever this library's readers find worth marking and no
parser will ever produce — *book club*, *for the cottage*, *unfinished*,
*worth rereading*. Anyone signed in can add and remove them, and everyone sees
the same ones.

Names match case-insensitively, so "Book club" and "book club" are one tag
rather than two; the first spelling used is the one kept. A tag that nothing
carries any more disappears by itself.

A tag has its own page and its own OPDS feed, so a shared vocabulary is
something you can browse from an e-reader too.

Where registration is open to strangers an administrator can close editing
without hiding the tags — they stay visible and searchable, they just stop
being editable.

## Lists

Yours. A list is a deliberate grouping that means something only to whoever made
it: *summer*, *to lend to Kate*, *the good Le Carrés*. Make one from the picker
on any book card.

A list is **private unless you share it**. Sharing grants reading, never
writing — nobody can add to or delete someone else's list, and a private list
answers "not found" rather than "forbidden", so its existence is not confirmed
either.

Shared lists appear on the **Lists** page and in the OPDS feed for everyone,
labelled with whose they are. Deleting a list never deletes the books on it.
